Screen 1 · Home

The feed is a wall of invitations

  • The 4-slot grid is the hero of every card — faces first, metadata second. An open slot is a dashed ring in its part color saying “Sing the Bass,” never an empty grey box.
  • Filter by what matters to a singer: the part that's needed, the voicing, difficulty. One tap from “I'm a bass” to a list of tags that need one.
  • Tag titles in songbook serif (New York on device) — the one typographic signature.
  • Brass + button is Start-a-Tag, always one tap away.

Screen 2 · Tag Detail

One tag, played big

  • The grid plays as one video (composite-so-far). The open slot stays visibly open during playback — the product's whole pitch in one frame.
  • Takes: multiple people can sing an open part; dots show 2 bass takes already waiting for the creator to feature. Curation without gatekeeping.
  • The join CTA is painted in the part's color — bass green here — and names the part, not a generic “Join.”
  • Share exports the collage with watermark → the growth loop.

Screen 3 · Record Setup

The pitch pipe is the centerpiece

  • A real, tappable brass pitch pipe — the one skeuomorphic indulgence, because it's the object every barbershopper reaches for first. Tap it, hear your key.
  • Voicing presets (TTBB / SSAA / Mixed) and part pick with the color system — your part is stamped “you.”
  • Key selection is explicit: everyone who joins later locks to this key automatically.
  • The record flow is always dark, in both app themes — backstage lighting, camera-friendly.

Screen 4 · Recording

Sing with the quartet that's already there

  • Your camera fills your quadrant, framed in record red; the other parts play in theirs while you sing — you're joining a performance, not recording into a void.
  • Serif count-in numerals over the grid after the pitch: 4 clicks, then everyone's singing.
  • Red is used only for recording state — dot, ring, frame. Nowhere else in the app.
  • Pre-flight (previous step) already confirmed earbuds, so the guide mix never bleeds into the mic.

Screen 5 · Sync Check

Nudge until the chord locks

  • Auto latency compensation gets close; the nudge slider (±250 ms) makes perfect — looped playback responds instantly as you drag.
  • Waveforms in part colors let you see the alignment against the lead, not just hear it.
  • “Sounds locked ✓” — the copy speaks barbershop. Submitting uploads your take and rebuilds the collage on-device.
  • Re-record is guilt-free and one tap; takes aren't published until you say so.
What to react to: the warmth of the ivory/plum/brass identity, the part-color system, the songbook serif on tag titles, the always-dark record flow, and the “Sing the Bass” framing of empty slots. Everything here is cheap to change now and expensive to change after Checkpoint 3 — see docs/06-decisions-needed.md, question 11.
